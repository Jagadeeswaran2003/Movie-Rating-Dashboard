# Movie Rating Dashboard
The dashboard titled "Movie Analysis Using Movie Rating" presents a comprehensive view of movie trends over several years. It highlights that films like Pirates of the Caribbean and Spider-Man 3 had the highest budgets, pointing to blockbuster-level investments. A clear positive correlation exists between audience and Rotten Tomatoes ratings, indicating that well-received movies tend to impress both critics and viewers. From 2007 to 2011, ratings have generally shown an upward trend, with 2011 marking the highest ratings. The genre distribution over the years reveals that Action, Drama, and Comedy dominated movie production. Each year shows a consistent volume of genre diversity, supporting a broad audience appeal. The "Genre wise Top 3 Movies" section shows Comedy leading in ratings, followed closely by Action and Drama. These genres likely reflect what audiences enjoy most. Overall, the dashboard captures trends in movie production, budget allocation, audience preferences, and critical reception. It serves as a useful tool for analyzing the dynamics of the film industry over time.
